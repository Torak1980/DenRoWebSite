Company Profile

High Tide Trade 6cc trading as DenRo Construction was established in 2004 with the desire to contribute to the growth of the country by creating employment opportunities whilst realizing sustainable growth for the company.

To realize this goal DenRo Construction actively tendered for work from Government, Corporate and Private Clients.

In order to be considered for tenders, DenRo Construction is compliant with and/or has registered with the following organisations:

  • Construction Industry Development Board (CIDB)
    • Grading of 6GBPE (General Building Category)
    • Grading of 2CE (Civil Works Category)
  • National Home Builders Registration Council
  • Letter of Good Standing issued by the Compensation Commissioner
  • Occupational Health and Safety requirements
  • Valid Tax Clearance Certificate issued by the South African Revenue Services
  • Central Supplier Data Base
  • BBBEE Level 1

To this end, structures are in place that will provide efficient and effective completion of development projects
